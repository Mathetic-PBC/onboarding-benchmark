Berkeley chats — what to ask


not a sales call. 30 min. listen

  1. walk me through the last time you had an idea for your research. where did you write it down first?
  2. how many places do your papers live? (zotero, downloads, slack, email…)
  3. when you go back to an old idea, how do you find where it came from?
  4. show me your notes app right now (if ok)
  5. what did you try and stop using? why?
  6. would you let a tool read your chats with claude/chatgpt?
