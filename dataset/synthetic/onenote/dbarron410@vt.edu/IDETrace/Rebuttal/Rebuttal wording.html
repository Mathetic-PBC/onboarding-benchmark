While running the ablation requested by R2 we found that our original train/test split was made at the window level, so windows from the same student appeared in both sets. We have re-split the data grouped by student id (no student appears in more than one split) and re-ran all experiments with five seeds. Our best model's macro-F1 changes from 0.81 to 0.74; all baselines drop by similar margins and the ranking of models is unchanged. We report both numbers in the revision, release the exact split ids, and add a course-held-out evaluation (0.69). We thank R2: the ablation is what exposed the problem.
