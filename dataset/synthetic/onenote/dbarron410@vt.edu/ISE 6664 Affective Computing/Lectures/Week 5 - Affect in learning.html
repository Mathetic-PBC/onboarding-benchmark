wk5 — affect + learning (Feb 10)

  • learning-centered states differ from basic emotions: confusion, frustration, boredom, engaged concentration, delight, surprise
  • confusion is not bad by itself — productive if it gets resolved
  • D'Mello & Graesser affect dynamics model
    • impasse → confusion
    • confusion unresolved → frustration
    • frustration persists → boredom / disengagement
    • resolution at any point → back to engaged concentration
  • implication: timing of help matters — intervene in the confusion→frustration transition, not before

this is basically the stalled-after-error state from the VizPI labeling guide v3. a student who errors, re-runs the same thing, then goes idle = confusion turning into frustration. worth a sentence in the related work for TutorTrace too.

Brooks mentioned Baker et al. "better to be frustrated than bored" — boredom is the one that predicts worse outcomes.
