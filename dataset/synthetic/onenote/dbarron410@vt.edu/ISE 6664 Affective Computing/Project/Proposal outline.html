proposal — due Feb 20

title: Frustration signals in AI-tutoring transcripts

RQs

  1. which observable conversational behaviours mark student frustration in LLM-tutor chats?
  2. how reliably can human coders label frustration at the turn level?
  3. do tutor response types (direct answer, hint, question, encouragement) differ in what follows a frustrated turn?

method

  • sample 200 of the ~600 transcripts, stratified by length
  • codebook: 5 states (neutral, confused, frustrated, disengaged, satisfied) + behavioural markers
  • 3 coders (us), 50 transcripts overlap for kappa
  • baseline: LIWC negemo per turn; ours: marker features + previous-turn context
  • transition analysis à la D'Mello & Graesser

risks

  • frustration might be rare (<10% turns) → class imbalance
  • stats homework ≠ programming, generalization caveat
  • only 3 coders, all on the team (bias)
