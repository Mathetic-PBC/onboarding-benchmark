Calvo & D'Mello (2010) — affect detection review

my summary: survey of detection across modalities (face, voice, body, physiology, text, multimodal). main argument is that the field had grown by modality silos and needed theory to drive which signals matter.

  • multimodal fusion helps less than expected in naturalistic data — gains mostly in posed datasets
  • most systems trained on acted emotions; spontaneous data much harder
  • calls for context-aware detection (task, person, history)

response angle

16 years later the silo problem looks the same for LLM tutoring: people classify single messages without the session context. that is the critique I wrote.
