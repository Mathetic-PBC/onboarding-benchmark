postmortem, dep #2 reconnect bug:
• cause: `onReconnect` didn't flush the client queue; the section B subnet kills idle sockets after ~60s
• window: 3:30–4:10pm, section B only (44 students)
• loss: 19,412 of ~165,000 expected section B events ≈ 11.8% (server rows diffed against client seq numbers from the post-fix flush). per-bucket numbers in the csv
• recoverable: no, except 9 students whose tabs stayed open, their queues flushed after the hotfix
• fix: flush on reconnect + persist the queue to localStorage (`a91d0f3`, `c3d07aa`)
• for analysis: flag section B windows that overlap a seq gap as `partial_telemetry=true`, don't drop them
