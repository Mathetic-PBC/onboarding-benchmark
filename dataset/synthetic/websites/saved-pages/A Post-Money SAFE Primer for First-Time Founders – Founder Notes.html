A Post-Money SAFE Primer for First-Time Founders

By Claire Ostend, Esq. · November 11, 2025

A SAFE is a right to future equity. With a post-money valuation cap, the investor's ownership at conversion is fixed: investment divided by the post-money cap.

InvestmentPost-money capOwnership at conversion
$100,000$8,000,0001.25%
$50,000$8,000,0000.625%
$750,000 total$8,000,0009.375%

Modeling it

Keep a cap table with separate tabs for founders, SAFEs, the option pool, and scenarios. Use formulas, not typed numbers, so a change to the cap flows through.

Things founders miss

  • SAFE dilution falls entirely on existing holders, including the option pool
  • A priced round may ask for the pool to be topped up before the new money, which dilutes founders again
  • Side letters (pro rata rights, information rights) matter more than they look
