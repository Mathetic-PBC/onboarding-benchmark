Delaware PBC Annual Reports, Explained

By Claire Ostend, Esq. · January 20, 2026

A Delaware corporation files an annual report and pays franchise tax by March 1 each year. A public benefit corporation (PBC) has the same filing plus a benefit statement to stockholders at least every two years.

The annual report

Filed online. Lists directors and the principal place of business. Late filing carries a penalty plus interest on unpaid franchise tax.

Franchise tax

The default "authorized shares" method can produce an alarming number for startups with millions of authorized shares. The "assumed par value capital" method usually yields a much smaller amount; you must choose it when filing.

The benefit statement

Describes the objectives the board set to promote the stated public benefit, the standards used to measure progress, and an assessment.

Put March 1 in your calendar now. Founders miss it most often in their first year.
