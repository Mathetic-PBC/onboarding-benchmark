HDBSCAN Parameters for Text Embeddings

By Arun Venkatesan · August 14, 2025

HDBSCAN is a good default for clustering sentence embeddings because it does not need the number of clusters and it labels outliers as noise.

Reduce first

Density estimates are unreliable in 384 or 768 dimensions. Reduce to 5-15 dimensions with UMAP (cosine metric) before clustering.

min_cluster_size

The smallest group you would care about. For a few thousand forum posts, 10-25 is a reasonable range. Too small gives hundreds of micro-clusters.

min_samples

Controls how conservative the clustering is. Higher values push more points into noise. Start at 5 and look at what lands in noise; short replies like "thanks" should.

Labeling clusters

Language models write decent cluster labels from a sample of posts, but check every label by hand. Generic labels like "questions about the assignment" usually hide two or three real topics.
