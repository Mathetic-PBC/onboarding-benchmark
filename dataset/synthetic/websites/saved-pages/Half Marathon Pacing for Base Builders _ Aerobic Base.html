Half Marathon Pacing for Base Builders

By Marta Lindgren · August 19, 2026

Runners who build mostly on easy volume often race the half too conservatively or, more often, go out at 10k effort because the first kilometers feel absurdly easy.

The plan

  • First 5 km: 3-5 seconds per kilometer slower than goal pace
  • 5-15 km: goal pace, fuel at roughly 35 and 70 minutes
  • 15 km to the finish: race whoever is near you

Choosing a goal pace

Use a recent 10k: multiply the time by about 2.21 for a well-trained base runner. A 37:00 10k suggests roughly 1:21-1:22; a base-heavy runner without much threshold work should add a minute or two.

Hills

Pace by effort on climbs. A short hill late in the race costs ten seconds whether you fight it or not; fighting it costs you the next kilometer too.

A negative split is the best evidence that the plan was right.
