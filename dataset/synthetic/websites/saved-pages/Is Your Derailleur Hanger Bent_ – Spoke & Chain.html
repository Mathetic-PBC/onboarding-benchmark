Is Your Derailleur Hanger Bent?

By Lou Ferreira · September 15, 2024

If your shifting went bad right after a crash or after the bike fell on its drive side, suspect the hanger before the derailleur.

Symptoms

  • Skipping in the middle cogs under load
  • Indexing that will not settle with the barrel adjuster
  • The derailleur cage looks tilted from behind

Quick check

Stand behind the bike and sight down the cassette. The jockey wheels should sit in a vertical line under the cog.

Straighten or replace

Aluminum hangers can be straightened once with an alignment tool. If it is badly bent or has been straightened before, replace it; they are cheap and designed to fail before your frame does.
