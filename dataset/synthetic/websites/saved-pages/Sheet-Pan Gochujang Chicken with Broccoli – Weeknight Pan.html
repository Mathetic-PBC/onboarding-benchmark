Sheet-Pan Gochujang Chicken with Broccoli

By Hye-jin Park · March 4, 2025

Ingredients (4 servings)

  • 900 g boneless chicken thighs
  • 3 tbsp gochujang
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 1 tbsp sesame oil
  • 2 cloves garlic, grated
  • 2 heads broccoli
  • 1 tbsp neutral oil, salt

Method

Heat the oven to 220°C (425°F). Mix gochujang, soy, honey, sesame oil and garlic. Toss the chicken in two-thirds of the sauce. Toss broccoli with oil and salt. Roast everything on one pan for 22-25 minutes, then brush the chicken with the remaining sauce and broil for 2 minutes.

Keeps four days in the fridge. Serve with rice and something crunchy.
