Sidechain Compression in FL Studio, Step by Step

By Kenji Morales · March 3, 2026

Sidechaining ducks one sound when another plays. In beat-making it usually means the pad or bass dips each time the kick hits, which clears space in the low end and adds a pulse.

Routing

  • Put the kick and the pad on separate mixer tracks
  • On the kick's mixer track, right-click the arrow on the pad's track and choose "Sidechain to this track only"
  • Add Fruity Limiter to the pad track and switch it to COMP mode

Settings to start with

  • SIDECHAIN input: select the kick's track number
  • Threshold low enough that the gain reduction meter moves 4-8 dB on each kick
  • Attack near zero; release 80-150 ms, adjusted to the tempo

Hearing it

Solo the pad with the kick muted: the ducking should still be audible, because the sidechain listens to the kick even when you cannot hear it. Too much release makes the pad sound like it is gasping. At slower tempos (80-90 bpm) a longer release sounds more natural.
