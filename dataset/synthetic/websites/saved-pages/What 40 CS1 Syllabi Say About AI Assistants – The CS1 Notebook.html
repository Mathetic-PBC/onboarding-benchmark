What 40 CS1 Syllabi Say About AI Assistants

By Dana Whitfield · October 8, 2025

I collected public syllabi from 40 introductory programming courses for Fall 2025 and coded their AI-assistant policies.

PolicyCourses
Banned outright6
Allowed with disclosure17
Allowed, course-provided assistant only11
No policy stated6

Course-provided assistants are rising

Eleven courses now ship their own assistant, usually a wrapped model with a system prompt that refuses to write full solutions. These courses also tend to collect usage logs, which raises questions about consent that only three syllabi addressed.

Disclosure is vague

Most "allowed with disclosure" policies ask students to mention AI use in a comment, without saying what level of detail counts.

Labs versus homework

Seven courses allow assistants in labs but not on homework. The logic is that labs have TAs present to notice over-reliance. Whether TAs can actually notice is an open question.
