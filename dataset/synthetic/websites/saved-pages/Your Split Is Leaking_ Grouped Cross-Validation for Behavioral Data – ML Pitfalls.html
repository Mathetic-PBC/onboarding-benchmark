Your Split Is Leaking: Grouped Cross-Validation for Behavioral Data

By Arun Venkatesan · October 30, 2025

If your dataset has many samples per person (windows of a session, frames of a video, days of a log), a random train/test split puts the same person on both sides. The model learns the person, not the behavior.

The symptom

Suspiciously high scores that drop sharply on new users. Rare classes look easier than they should.

The fix

Split by group. In scikit-learn: GroupKFold, StratifiedGroupKFold, or GroupShuffleSplit, with the person id as the group. Then assert that the intersection of train and test ids is empty.

Reporting

If you already published or submitted with a leaky split, report both numbers and say which one readers should trust. Reviewers respond better to a visible correction than to a quiet one.
